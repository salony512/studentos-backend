Mindful Activities

Keep the brain
lighter every day

Not decoration — these activities are scientifically positioned to reduce cognitive fatigue, prevent the crash cycle, and reset your focus when it matters.

🌬️
4-7-8 breath timer

Inhale 4s, hold 7s, exhale 8s. Activates the parasympathetic nervous system and drops cortisol in under 4 minutes. Auto-triggers after 90-min study blocks.

4 min · Anytime
🧠
Brain dump journal

30-second voice or text dump before starting work. Clears background anxiety from working memory so your full cognitive bandwidth goes to the task.

2 min · Pre-session
⭐
Daily intention setter

One question every morning: "What would make today feel successful?" Sets a meaningful anchor — not a task list. Prevents the overwhelm spiral before it starts.

1 min · Morning
🌿
Nature micro-break

Step outside or look at something green for 5 minutes. Proven to restore directed attention better than social media scrolling. Scheduled into your day plan automatically.

5 min · Midday
🌸
Gratitude micro-log

3 things in 60 seconds every evening. The AI tracks themes over time and shows what energizes vs. drains you — building self-awareness quietly in the background.

1 min · Evening
🎵
Adaptive focus mode

Pomodoro timer with AI-selected ambient background — rain, cafe, lo-fi, white noise — matched to your task type and current energy level, not just a random playlist.
